# native-learning

Daily 30–60 min lessons taking a React Native developer to SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose.

- Workflow and rules: `.agents/skills/daily-native-lesson/SKILL.md` (load it for any lesson-related request).
- Fresh clone: run `scripts/init.sh` before reading learner state. It creates ignored `LESSONS.md` from `templates/LESSONS.template.md` without replacing an existing log.
- Memory: `LESSONS.md` is the only record of what the learner knows. Do not infer knowledge from other folders, repos, or sessions.
- Privacy: never commit `LESSONS.md`, local agent/IDE settings, credentials, or personal planning notes. Shared lesson folders and curriculum links are materials, not evidence of completion.
- Plan: `CURRICULUM.md`. Toolkit and conventions: `README.md`. Templates: `templates/`. Scripts: `scripts/`.
- Lessons are concept-first and design-system-neutral (no CDS lens).
- Don't commit build output (`.build/`, `build/`, `.gradle/`, `*.xcodeproj` are generated by the scripts).
